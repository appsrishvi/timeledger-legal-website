1. Who we are

TimeLedger ("the app") is a personal time-tracking app for Android.

Controller / developer: Rishvi Ltd

Contact for privacy questions and requests: support@rishvi.co.uk

3. Why we use it

  • To let you sign in and keep your data tied to your account.
  • To provide time tracking, goals, plans, timeline, insights and reports.
  • To keep your data on your devices in sync.
  • To send you account emails (sign-up confirmation and password-recovery codes).

We do not sell your information and the app contains no advertising.

4. Storage on your device

Your activities, sessions, goals and plans are stored in a database on your phone, separately for each account. The app works offline. The local database is not additionally encrypted by the app; it relies on Android's app sandbox. Reports you export (PDF/CSV) are created on your phone and only leave it if you share them. Android backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this app, so this data is not copied to Google backups or to a new phone by Android.

5. Cloud synchronisation

When you are signed in and online, your account data is uploaded to our backend, which is hosted on Supabase (database, authentication and file storage). Data is sent over HTTPS. Database access is restricted per user with row-level security, and your profile photo is stored in a private storage area that only your account can read. If the same item is changed on two devices, the app shows both and you choose which to keep.

7. Security

  • Connections to the backend use HTTPS.
  • The app only contains a public (publishable) backend key; no administrative keys are in the app.
  • Each user's data is isolated on the device and on the server.
  • Account deletion is performed by a server function that checks you are signed in and can only delete your own account.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. How long we keep information

  • While your account exists, your data is kept so the app can work and sync.
  • When you delete your account, your profile photo, profile, preferences, activities, categories, sessions, pauses, goals, plans and sync-device records are removed from the backend in the same operation.
  • Our service providers (Supabase and Brevo) may keep backups, logs and email delivery records for limited periods under their own policies.
  • Files you exported and saved or shared outside the app are under your control and are not deleted by account deletion.

9. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account. Email accounts confirm with their password; Google accounts confirm with Google. The data on the phone is removed after the server confirms the deletion.
  • Without the app: see the account-deletion page for how to request deletion.

10. Your choices and rights

You can view and correct your information in the app, delete sessions, activities and goals, and delete your account. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights (access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, complaint to a regulator). To exercise them contact support@rishvi.co.uk.

11. Children

TimeLedger is not directed at children.
