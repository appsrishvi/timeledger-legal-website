Delete your TimeLedger account

You can delete your account and its data yourself in the app, or ask us to do it if you can no longer open the app.

Option 1: Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open TimeLedger and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm: enter your password (email accounts) or confirm with Google (Google accounts).

Deletion is performed on our server. The app removes the data stored on your phone only after the server confirms the deletion. You need an internet connection.

Option 2: Ask us to delete it

Use this if you uninstalled the app, lost your phone, or cannot sign in.

  1. Send an email to support@rishvi.co.uk with the subject "Delete my TimeLedger account".
  2. Send it from the email address registered to your TimeLedger account. This is how we confirm the request is yours. Google Sign-In users: use the Google account email.
  3. Do not include your password. We will never ask for it.
  4. We will confirm by email when the account has been deleted.

If the request comes from a different address, we may ask you to prove you own the account before we delete anything.

What is not deleted

  • PDF or CSV reports you exported and saved or shared outside the app. You control those files.
  • Records held by our service providers (Supabase and Brevo), such as backups and email delivery logs, for their own limited periods.

Is deletion reversible?

No. Once your account is deleted, it cannot be restored. If you want to keep a copy of your time records first, export a report from Settings → Export reports.
